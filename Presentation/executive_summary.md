# Executive Summary — TradeMorph StrategyLab

## Vision
TradeMorph StrategyLab aims to be the **on-ramp for manual traders** into algorithmic trading. The product focuses on turning familiar checklist-style strategies into algo skeletons, providing safe backtesting, and enabling a gradual automation journey.

---

## Market opportunity
- **Retail base:** ~3M Indian traders with consistent manual habits  
- **Adoption gap:** <15% currently automate any part of their process  
- **Trend tailwinds:** SEBI openness to tech adoption, rising smartphone penetration, growing appetite for no-code tools  
- **Estimated TAM:** ~₹900 Cr+ by 2028, CAGR >50%  

---

## Differentiation
Unlike incumbents that are either:
- *Beginner-friendly but locked-in* (e.g. broker add-ons), or  
- *Powerful but intimidating* (e.g. quant dev platforms),  

TradeMorph positions itself uniquely:  
1. **Conversion-first workflow** — start from manual trades, not blank screens  
2. **Export-first ethos** — readable Python skeletons, no vendor lock-in  
3. **Progressive automation** — manual → hybrid alerts → auto execution  
4. **Education built-in** — templates, guided flows, and in-app learning  

---

## Product pillars
- **Strategy Wizard**: 4-step guided builder (describe → entry/exit → risk → preview code)  
- **Backtest Console**: instant equity curve, P&L stats, realistic slippage costs  
- **Execution Modes**: manual, hybrid, auto — with confidence scores and overrides  
- **Community Templates** (future): library of starter strategies, shareable JSON configs  

---

## Business model
- **Freemium**: core wizard + 3 backtests/month free  
- **Pro (₹499/mo)**: unlimited backtests, multi-symbol, export history  
- **Marketplace**: commission on premium strategy templates (launch later)  

---

## Year 1 targets
- Users: 30k active; 1.8k paying (~6% conversion)  
- Revenue: ~₹1.1 Cr (conservative)  
- NPS > 50  
- Activation metric: >70% of new users run at least one backtest within first week  

---

## Risks & mitigations
- **Tech risk:** browser backtests may be slow → start with small datasets + server fallback  
- **Perception risk:** users may expect “ready-to-trade” algos → strong disclaimers + transparency in code  
- **Competition risk:** replication likely → defend via UX, education, community trust  

---

## Roadmap snapshot
- **Q1–Q2:** MVP launch — wizard + basic backtester + export  
- **Q3:** Add hybrid execution and template library  
- **Q4:** Launch Pro plan, scale partnerships with educators/brokers  

---

## Investment ask
- **Requirement:** ₹5 Cr seed (12-month runway)  
- **Use of funds:** 45% product/tech, 30% GTM/education, 15% infra, 10% compliance  
- **Expected outcome:** >₹1 Cr ARR, validated PMF, foundation for Series A  

---

## Conclusion
TradeMorph StrategyLab is positioned to bridge the largest gap in retail trading: converting proven manual habits into safe, transparent algorithmic workflows. With a lean MVP, freemium funnel, and strong UX differentiation, we believe this initiative can establish **category leadership** in India’s fast-growing algo trading market.
