## Executive Summary
This submission analyzes OptiFlow’s trading platform and proposes enhancements to strengthen its competitive positioning. Core deliverables include **competitor analysis**, **UI/UX improvements**, and a **Strategy Converter feature** that enables manual traders to transition into algorithmic trading.

---

## Competitor Analysis
**TradingView** → Large community, intuitive charts, but limited automation  
**QuantConnect** → Powerful, multi-asset, but steep learning curve  
**MetaTrader** → Wide broker support, but outdated UX  
**TrendSpider** → Automated pattern recognition, but expensive  

**Gap identified:** A platform that simplifies **manual → algo transition** without coding.
