# Revenue Projections — TradeMorph StrategyLab (practical, conservative forecast)

**Assumptions (MVP-first, India-focused):**
- Product launches as Freemium with a Pro monthly plan.
- Lean marketing & partnership push in Year 1.
- Monetization focuses on subscriptions and template marketplace.

---

## Pricing model (simple)
- **Free** — core converter, 3 free backtests/month
- **Pro** — ₹499 / month (unlimited backtests, multi-symbol, export history)
- **Template marketplace** — 30% platform fee on paid templates (launch later)

---

## User growth assumptions (conservative)
- Month 0 (private beta): 500 users
- Month 3: 5,000 users (launch + content push)
- Month 12: 50,000 users (organic + partnerships)
- Paid conversion: start at 4% (Month 3) → 8% by Month 12 with product tweaks

---

## Year 1 projection (conservative)
- Average active users in Year 1: 30,000
- Paid conversion average: 6% → paying users ≈ 1,800
- Annualized ARR from subscriptions:
  - 1,800 × ₹499 × 12 = **₹10.77 lakhs** × 10? — clarify: compute properly below

**Detailed calculation:**
- Monthly paying users (average) ≈ 1,800  
- Monthly subscription revenue ≈ 1,800 × ₹499 = ₹8,98,200  
- Year 1 subscription revenue ≈ ₹8,98,200 × 12 = **₹1,07,78,400 (~₹1.08 Cr)**

- Marketplace & other revenue (conservative): ₹5,00,000  
- **Total Year 1 revenue (conservative estimate): ~₹1.13 Cr**

---

## Year 2 projection (scale, moderate)
- Active users: 150,000
- Paid conversion: 8% → paying users = 12,000
- Monthly sub revenue: 12,000 × ₹499 = ₹59,88,000
- Year 2 subscription revenue: ₹59,88,000 × 12 = **₹7.19 Cr**
- Marketplace & premium templates: ₹50 lakh
- **Total Year 2 revenue estimate: ~₹7.69 Cr**

---

## Year 3 projection (mature)
- Active users: 300,000
- Paid conversion: 10% → paying users = 30,000
- Monthly sub revenue: 30,000 × ₹499 = ₹1.497 Cr
- Year 3 subscription revenue: ₹1.497 Cr × 12 = **₹17.96 Cr**
- Marketplace, enterprise blurbs, partnerships: ₹2.5 Cr
- **Total Year 3 revenue estimate: ~₹20.5 Cr**

---

## Unit economics & CAC (target)
- **CAC (target):** ₹800–1,500 via content/partnership channels (initially higher)  
- **LTV:** assume average paid lifetime 18 months → LTV = ₹499 × 18 ≈ ₹8,982 (~₹9k)  
- **LTV/CAC target:** ≥ 6x (healthy SaaS for small teams)

---

## Sensitivity & scenarios
- **Downside (50% adoption):**
  - Year 1: ₹0.56 Cr
  - Year 2: ₹3.85 Cr
  - Year 3: ₹10.25 Cr
- **Upside (faster conversion + marketplace traction):**
  - Year 1: ₹2–3 Cr
  - Year 2: ₹12–15 Cr
  - Year 3: ₹35–45 Cr (with enterprise and broker partnerships)

---

## Conclusion & recommendations
- Aim for a **low price point** in the Indian market to rapidly build paying base. ₹499/month is intentionally conservative.
- Early focus should be growth and engagement; monetization experiments (monthly vs yearly, student discounts) can follow after product-market-fit signals.
- Marketplace and enterprise channels are high-leverage — prioritize them in Years 2–3 once product has stable usage.
